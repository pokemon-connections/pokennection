HOW TO PLAY

How Does Pokémon Connections Work?

Each Pokennection puzzle gives you 16 Pokémon. Your goal is to identify four sets of four Pokémon that share a hidden connection. The relationship might involve types, generations, evolutions, visual traits, Pokédex facts, or another part of the Pokémon world.

1

SCAN THE BOARD

Look through all 16 Pokémon before making your first guess.

2

FIND A LINK

Choose four Pokémon that you believe share the same hidden connection.

3

SUBMIT

Submit your group. Correct answers reveal a complete category.

4

SOLVE ALL 4

Discover every category before you run out of lives.

FOR POKÉMON & PUZZLE FANS

Pokémon Connections, Trivia and Logic in One Game

If you enjoy Pokémon trivia, daily puzzle games, logic challenges or Connections-style games, Pokennection turns Pokémon knowledge into a quick daily brain teaser.

DAILY POKÉMON CONNECTIONS

Every puzzle challenges you to find relationships between Pokémon that may come from different generations, types or evolution families.

The strongest strategy is often to identify the most specific relationship first, then use elimination to solve the remaining groups.

POKÉMON CONNECTIONS UNLIMITED

Finished today's game? Unlimited mode lets you continue playing more Pokémon connection puzzles without waiting for tomorrow.

It's a useful way to practice pattern recognition and test how deep your Pokémon knowledge really goes.
